World news, Wednesday 11 November 2026. Leaders of 40-plus European countries met in Dublin for the European Political Community summit on Ukraine's security and ending the war with Russia (AP); US-mediated Russia-Ukraine talks in Abu Dhabi ended their second day without a breakthrough, though a prisoner exchange was agreed (BBC, Global News), and Ukraine struck targets deep inside Russia (ABC News).

At the COP31 climate summit in Antalya, world leaders took the stage (Reuters) after 93 private jets flew in, drawing criticism (CNN); an AP analysis found five years of climate diplomacy have not slowed global warming, and the IEA warned of severe winter energy pressures in Ukraine (Reuters).

The Wall Street Journal reported, citing two sources, that Nvidia's board is preparing to oust CEO and co-founder Jensen Huang — an unconfirmed report that sank the stock in after-hours trade (CNBC) and drew a call from Congress for a national-security investigation (Fox Business). Days earlier the board had ordered an independent review. US stocks recovered from the tech rout, the S&P 500 up 1.3% and the Nasdaq 1.6% (Reuters).
